PAR CANNON

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 4, 2026

This explains what Par Cannon collects, why, and who it's shared with. We don't sell your data and there are no ads.

What we collect

Account: your email address (or Apple sign-in ID), name, username and handicap index, plus your home course if you add one.

Golf: rounds, scores and stats, shots (with where each shot started and finished), games, stakes, side games and settle-up records, and greens you mark on the map.

Social: your friends and friend requests.

Location

Your location is used to show distances during a round, to track shots you record, and to find courses near you. It is only used while you are playing a round or searching for courses. Shot positions you save are stored with your round; your live position is not stored.

Who can see it

People in a round with you see that round's scores, games and ledger. Other signed-in users can find your username, name and handicap so they can add you as a friend. Greens you mark are shared with everyone playing that course.

Services we use

Supabase (database, sign-in and storage, hosted in the US), Brevo (sign-in emails), Apple (Sign in with Apple and app delivery), OpenStreetMap (course layouts and nearby courses, requested from your phone with the map area), Open-Meteo (wind and elevation for the course location), and GolfCourseAPI (course scorecards, requested by our server). They get only what they need to do that job.

Keeping and deleting data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can edit your profile in Settings at any time. To delete your account and data, ask us (see "Changes and contact") and we will delete it. An in-app delete button is coming before the public release.

Children

Par Cannon isn't meant for children under 13, and money-stake games are for adults 18+.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we'll update the date at the top. Questions or deletion requests: send them to the developer with TestFlight's feedback (take a screenshot in the app, then tap Share Beta Feedback).
